Phillip Critchfield

Training Manager, HVTS Americas

Phillip Critchfield began his career with Metalspray in 1993 as a technician, supporting on-site thermal spray operations across the globe. Prior to his career in the industry, Phillip served on active duty in the U.S. Army from June 1979 to February 1989.

Since joining the field, Phillip has built extensive experience in field operations, safety oversight, project supervision, and technical training. Over the years, he has supported projects across the U.S. and internationally, including work in power plants, pulp and paper mills, and oil refineries. His experience includes overseeing sandblasting and the application of erosion- and corrosion-protection materials, as well as supporting broader health, environment, and safety programs.

In his current training role, Phillip brings a strong combination of hands-on field knowledge, safety expertise, and workforce development experience. He has played an important role in training both new and experienced team members, helping employees build the technical skills and safety awareness needed for successful field work.
